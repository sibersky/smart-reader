# Smart Reader — Teacher Alex AI Suite

**Smart Reader** is an AI-powered interactive graded reader that transforms dense, authentic English texts (articles, book excerpts, exam prompts, or screenshots) into personalized learning experiences tailored to the student's CEFR level.

## Core Features

- **Multimodal OCR & Text Analysis**: Upload screenshots/photos or paste text directly.
- **CEFR Difficulty Highlighting**:
  - **Borderline (+1 Level)**: Soft amber highlight for vocabulary just above the student's mastery level.
  - **Advanced (+2 Levels or higher)**: Soft coral/rose highlight for advanced vocabulary and idioms.
- **Interactive Gloss Card**:
  - Contextual simplified English definition.
  - One-click reveal/hide of translation into native language (Russian, Spanish, German, French, etc.).
  - Text-to-speech audio pronunciation.
  - Single-click flashcard copy (`Word — Translation // Definition`).
  - Direct addition to **Poke-Me-On** spaced repetition deck.
- **Comprehensive Vocabulary List**: Toggle between book reader view and full vocabulary table.
- **Export**: Download full vocabulary lists (.txt) formatted for instant Anki or Poke-Me-On import.
- **SSO Authentication**: Gated for registered Teacher Alex accounts with seamless login redirect.
